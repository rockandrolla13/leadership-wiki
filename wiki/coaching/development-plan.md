## Notes

- Drucker's feedback analysis works on a 9-12 month feedback loop. The development plan should reflect that horizon, not the quarterly OKR horizon. The two horizons coexist: OKRs for specific changes, feedback analysis for the deeper strength map.
- Christensen's warning: high-achievement people *unconsciously* allocate the marginal hour to whatever provides the most tangible accomplishment (career). The development plan should explicitly reserve time for non-career priorities (relationships, family, faith, craft) — they will not get the time by default.
- The "should" trap (McKee) is the most common form of off-plan drift. If the development plan starts to read like what other people would say the user *should* be doing, it has drifted.
