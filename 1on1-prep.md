# 1:1 Prep — Catch-Up

**This is a catch-up, not the review.** Goal: he talks, I listen. I aim to talk **under 40%** of the time.

**Setup:** Laptop closed. Phone face-down. No notes during — capture commitments for 30 seconds at the end.

## The 4 things I'm practicing

1. **WAIT** — before I speak, ask myself: *Why Am I Talking?*
2. **Silence** — after a real question, count to 20 before filling the gap. His real answer comes after the polite one.
3. **Follow up, don't move on** — "Tell me more about that." Three follow-ups beat three new topics.
4. **Recap before responding** — "Let me say back what I think you mean — tell me where I'm wrong."

## The questions — ask ONE, then follow up and stop. Don't run the list.

**Open (read mood, not status)**
- "How are you, actually?"
- "Before any work stuff — what's been on your mind this week?"

**His world**
- "What's taking more of your energy than it should right now?"
- "What's going well that I probably don't see?"

**Blockers / what's unsaid**
- "Where are you having to work around something to get things done?"
- "What are you handling on your own that I could make easier — or should just stay out of?"
- "Anything you've been holding back because it didn't seem worth raising?"

**Give him the wheel**
- "If you could change one thing about how we work together, what would it be?"
- "Do you have any feedback for me? Not asking you to make a case — if something I do makes your job harder, I want to hear it." → **then sit in the silence.**

**Close**
- "So you'll do X, I'll do Y — let's pick it up next week."

## Watch for

- The **pauses**, not the words.
- **One unprompted real thing** = trust opening. Answer with *"Glad you told me — what do you need?"* Never *"how did we let this happen?"*
- "No feedback" is normal for months. Don't push, don't read it as failure.

## Don't

- Jump to fixing before I understand it.
- Defend anything he gives me — investigate, restate, come back later.
- Fire the whole list. Menu, not script.
